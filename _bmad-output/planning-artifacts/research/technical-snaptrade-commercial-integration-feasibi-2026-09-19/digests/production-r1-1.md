# SnapTrade Commercial production feasibility — research digest

**Decision:** **Conditionally feasible, not an unconditional launch approval.** SnapTrade Commercial is explicitly intended for an application managing many end users' brokerage connections, but production requires KYC/payment/production-access approval and compliance with SnapTrade's read-enabled policy. A portfolio-informed matching service must be designed so raw brokerage/market data is used only to serve its originating licensed user and is never re-sold, re-packaged, or shared with other users. The published policy does **not** explicitly approve a matching/recommendation product or clarify whether derived match signals may be disclosed to another user; obtain SnapTrade's written approval before production launch and legal/privacy review for the app's own data use.

## Required launch gates

- Complete Commercial KYC, payment method, Production Access Application, and selected-broker approvals/BYO-key arrangements.
- Select data plan deliberately: real-time if the matching experience truly needs current holdings; otherwise Daily reduces freshness but adds manual-sync economics. Model connected-user billing even for disabled connections.
- Build secure server-side credential handling, explicit SnapTrade-terms assent evidence, data-deletion handling, a user-facing privacy notice/consent flow, and status/webhook-driven reconnect UX.
- Constrain matching to user-requested analysis and prevent raw market/brokerage data from serving any other user. Seek written SnapTrade confirmation for the exact derived-data/matching flow before launch; separately obtain counsel on applicable privacy, consumer-protection, securities/advice, and matchmaking-profile obligations.

## Gaps / limits of the public record

- No public official source found that specifically authorizes or bans **derived portfolio attributes** (for example, an opt-in investment-style match score) being shown to a different end user. The terms clearly restrict raw end-user and market data; this ambiguity is material.
- No public official fixed approval-SLA or complete production-application checklist was found; individual broker approval status and access can change.
- No official Vercel documentation was researched because the question is SnapTrade-commercial feasibility; SnapTrade's server-side key/encryption requirement applies regardless of host. Hosting suitability needs a separate architecture/security review.
