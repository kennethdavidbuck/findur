# SnapTrade Commercial — data and event feasibility digest

**Decision answer:** **Qualified yes.** Commercial integration exposes sufficient user-authorized account, holdings, cash/buying-power, historical-activity, and order data to derive portfolio- and behavior-informed matching signals. It is unsuitable as the sole source for deterministic real-time execution-event signals or complete, uniform cross-broker history: transaction data is daily and one-day delayed; available fields/freshness depend on the brokerage; and relevant “holdings updated” events report sync attempt/completion, not a change diff. Treat signals as consented, snapshot/eventually-consistent features and retain provenance/freshness per account.

## Decision limits and open gaps

- **Not a push trade-feed:** official documentation found sync/lifecycle webhooks, not broker order-execution change events. Use `recentOrders` polling for near-real-time state; do not infer a webhook guarantees execution-level latency. **Confidence:** medium; class: evidence-bounded absence.
- **Brokerage variation:** the official freshness guide explicitly says availability/freshness depend on the brokerage. Validate the target broker set, including order timestamps, asset classes, tax lots, history depth, and disabled-connection recovery, with live test accounts. **Confidence:** high; class: documented limitation/recommendation.
- **Consent, retention, privacy, and compliance:** this pass did not establish whether SnapTrade commercial terms or applicable financial/privacy rules permit the proposed derived matching, sharing, profiling, retention, or marketing use. Resolve with SnapTrade commercial agreement/compliance review and counsel before production. **Confidence:** high that this is an unresolved scope; class: gap.
- **Refresh economics/quotas:** manual Daily refresh can incur charges, and commercial billing may include connected-user/API/platform/minimum charges; exact contracted limits/pricing were not established. **URLs:** https://docs.snaptrade.com/reference/Connections/Connections_refreshBrokerageAuthorization ; https://docs.snaptrade.com/docs/billing **Publisher:** SnapTrade. **Published:** not stated. **Accessed:** 2026-09-19. **Confidence:** high. **Class:** documented constraint/gap.
