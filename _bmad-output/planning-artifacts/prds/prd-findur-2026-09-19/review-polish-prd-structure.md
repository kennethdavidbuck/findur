# Structural Editorial Review — Findur PRD

**Purpose/audience read:** This document exists to help product, UX, architecture, engineering, and review teams align on and implement a safe, owner-only public demonstration of Findur.

**Structure model:** Strategic/Context (Pyramid). The review applies the Microsoft Writing Style Guide's human-reader priorities: clear hierarchy, direct navigation, and information ordered for scanning. Content and product decisions are unchanged; these are proposed editorial moves only.

**Word-metrics baseline:** 7,144 words total. No length target was supplied. Section counts cited below come from `word_metrics.py`.

## Summary

There are 9 recommendations: 5 moves/merges/condensations, 1 small navigation addition, 1 regrouping, and 2 explicit preserves. The document already has strong sectioning and complete traceability; its primary readability gain comes from front-loading scope, decision boundaries, and success criteria, then moving the glossary out of the main reading path.

If every reduction is accepted, the estimated net reduction is **247–343 words**, bringing the PRD from **7,144** to approximately **6,801–6,897 words** (**3.5–4.8%**). The small reading note adds 12–18 words and is included in that range. No length target was provided, so there is no target to assess. The only notable comprehension trade-off is that condensing feature introductions removes some repeated journey reinforcement; retain the proposed compact journey traceability cue and preserve §2.4 to avoid losing that orientation.
