# Structural Editorial Review — Findur PRD

**Purpose/audience read:** This document exists to help product, UX, architecture, engineering, and review teams agree on, implement, and accept a safe owner-only public demonstration of Findur without mistaking it for a public dating launch.

**Structure model:** Strategic/Context (Pyramid). The review uses the Microsoft Writing Style Guide for a human audience: put the decision and current status first, group related information, and make long technical material easy to scan. Product decisions and requirements are treated as fixed; every recommendation below changes only placement, grouping, or expression.

**Word-metrics baseline:** 8,841 words total. Section counts below come from `word_metrics.py`. No length target was supplied.

## Summary

There are **13 recommendations**: 5 moves, 6 condensations/merges, 1 regrouping, and 1 explicit preserve. The highest-impact fixes are to surface the external-hosting blocker in §0, move scope and success criteria ahead of detailed requirements, and remove repeated implementation detail from the journeys and feature introductions.

If every proposed reduction is accepted, the estimated net reduction is **630–840 words**, bringing the document from **8,841** to approximately **8,001–8,211 words** (**7.1–9.5%**). The 5–10-word blocker cross-reference, if retained, is included conservatively in this range. No length target was provided, so there is no target to assess.

The principal comprehension trade-offs are in shortening the journeys and moving the glossary. Preserve all seven journey outcomes, keep the glossary complete and easy to reach, and retain the suggested traceability mapping; otherwise, brevity would cost useful cross-functional orientation.
