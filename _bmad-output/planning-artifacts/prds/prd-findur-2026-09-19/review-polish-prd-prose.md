# Prose Editorial Review — Findur PRD

**Purpose/audience read:** This document exists to help product, UX, architecture, engineering, and review teams agree on, implement, and accept a safe owner-only public demonstration of Findur without mistaking it for a public dating launch.

**Structure model:** Strategic/Context (Pyramid), retained from the preceding structure review. This pass accounts for the structural edits already applied and does not reopen structure findings.

**Style and voice preserved:** Direct, precise, safety-conscious product language; defined product terms in title case; concise, testable requirement statements; parallel acceptance bullets; and explicit distinctions between the demonstration and a future public launch. The Microsoft Writing Style Guide is the baseline.

**Word-metrics baseline:** 8,793 words total. No length target was supplied.
