# Brief Reconciliation: Findur

**Input reviewed:** [Product Brief: Findur](../../briefs/brief-findur-2026-09-19/brief.md)
**Compared against:** [PRD](prd.md) and [PRD Addendum](addendum.md)
**Method:** Extracted gaps only. This review does not modify or supersede its inputs.

## Reconciliation result

The PRD and addendum carry forward the brief's core portfolio-first proposition, qualified verification claim, disclosure asymmetry, invisible cohort model, one-swipe photo reveal, owner-live/synthetic-candidate demonstration boundary, trust tone, and no-public-launch scope.

## Material gaps

1. **Usability outcomes omit perceived relevance and disclosure comfort.** The brief names perceived relevance of Candidate Cards, comfort with disclosure tiers, progression to mutual matches, and safety/trust concerns as important later testing signals. The PRD measures verification, disclosure comprehension, lifecycle clarity, and portfolio-first perception, but not whether users find cards relevant, feel comfortable with the disclosure model, or identify safety/trust concerns. Add formative measures or explicitly defer these experience outcomes.

2. **Playful shared-market relevance is not preserved as a bounded future direction.** The brief's vision allows shared experiences and market events to add playful relevance while avoiding performance exposure or ranking disclosure. The PRD defers dynamic market-moment signals and retains the general tone guardrails, but it does not preserve the positive “shared experience” opportunity or frame the condition under which it could graduate from a deferred option. Record it as a future UX/product exploration subject to the existing non-advisory, anti-shaming, and disclosure safeguards.

3. **The continuous/evolving-market vision is narrowed without an explicit handoff.** The brief describes an invisible candidate market that can evolve with portfolio characteristics, activity, engagement, proximity, preferences, disclosure, and market events. The PRD implements a ranked deck using the first six factors, while market-timed relevance is explicitly deferred; it does not say whether post-MVP deck refresh/evolution from user or market change is a deliberate product-direction capability. Add a future-direction note so this qualitative vision is not silently lost while keeping it outside the demonstration commitment.

## No-gap confirmations

- Portfolio-led cards, photo obscuring, and single-decision Mutual Match reveal are explicit PRD requirements.
- Open entry with portfolio-governed differentiated access, including the higher-disclosure initiation asymmetry, is represented in FR-6 and addendum §1.1–§1.2.
- The responsive, publicly hosted test-OAuth demonstration; one owner-controlled live portfolio; synthetic candidate population; chat stretch boundary; and prohibition on production credentials, public launch, trades, advice, wealth claims, and real candidate financial data are preserved.
- The requested emotional posture—fun enough for dating, professional enough for financial trust; calm, non-judgmental, anti-status, and non-hype—is preserved in PRD §9.3 and addendum §2.4.
