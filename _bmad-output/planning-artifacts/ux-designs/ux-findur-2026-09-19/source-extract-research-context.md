# UX Source Extract: Market and SnapTrade Research

This extract is primarily limited to the two named research artifacts. A dated spot-check against current official SnapTrade API documentation is recorded below where it validates or qualifies the field-level UX contract. **Requirement** means the source explicitly directs or recommends product behavior. **Research implication** means a UX consequence inferred from source facts; it remains a design decision. **Gap** means the sources do not resolve the issue.

## Product requirements stated by the sources

### Positioning, matching, and disclosure

- **Requirement:** Portfolio-derived inputs must materially affect candidate eligibility or ranking; the concept cannot be merely another dating filter or a decorative verification badge. Candidate cards may show only standardized, user-approved bands or traits. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#verdict`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`
- **Requirement:** Do not expose raw holdings, exact values, account identifiers, full performance, balances, positions, trades, security names, tickers, or financial scores to prospective matches. For the prototype, the live SnapTrade-backed view is private to its owner, while candidate profiles and the matching pool are synthetic. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/research/technical-snaptrade-commercial-integration-feasibi-2026-09-19/research.md#production-grade-build-constraints-and-future-product-boundary`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/research/technical-snaptrade-commercial-integration-feasibi-2026-09-19/research.md#recommendation`
- **Requirement:** Do not rank people by wealth, label anyone “verified wealthy,” assign an overall financial-worthiness or desirability score, or monetize access to a person's financial data. Disclosure tiers may govern what can be seen and who may initiate contact, with recipient protection and an explanation of why a candidate appears. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`
- **Requirement:** Matching language and explanations should emphasize opt-in derived traits—such as diversification preference or activity recency—and financial values such as habits, responsibility, goals, risk, and communication. Portfolio value alone is not an adequate proxy. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#trends-and-constraints-that-matter-to-the-prd`

### Consent, trust, and control

- **Requirement:** Treat consent as a lifecycle. Before connection, explain the precise data categories and purpose; separate private matching inputs from visible disclosure; show source coverage and last-refresh time; and support disconnect, revocation, deletion/retention handling, degraded access, and reauthorization. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`
- **Requirement:** Request the minimum OAuth/OIDC scopes needed for the prototype (`openid`, `email`, `read`, and `webhook` in the recommended flow), derive private features, and label their freshness. The user must have or create a SnapTrade Personal account and connect a brokerage through SnapTrade. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/research/technical-snaptrade-commercial-integration-feasibi-2026-09-19/research.md#authentication-and-onboarding`
- **Requirement:** Make revocation, deletion, and connection errors visible and testable. The implementation must also validate OAuth callback boundaries and protect client secrets, access tokens, and persisted credentials server-side; these controls should not depend on the UI keeping secrets. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/research/technical-snaptrade-commercial-integration-feasibi-2026-09-19/research.md#production-grade-build-constraints-and-future-product-boundary`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/research/technical-snaptrade-commercial-integration-feasibi-2026-09-19/research.md#recommendation`
- **Requirement:** Explain data use and sharing clearly, minimize collection/use/retention to the requested service, and do not use connected financial data for targeted advertising, cross-selling, sale, repackaging, or redistribution. The CFPB rules cited are a design bar rather than a confirmed legal conclusion for Findur; counsel must determine applicability. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#trends-and-constraints-that-matter-to-the-prd`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/research/technical-snaptrade-commercial-integration-feasibi-2026-09-19/research.md#production-grade-build-constraints-and-future-product-boundary`

### Freshness and reliability

- **Requirement:** Design the first release as eventually consistent and preserve source and freshness metadata with every derived feature. Do not promise live trades or interpret a holdings-updated webhook as proof that the portfolio changed; it reports a sync attempt/completion, not a diff. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/research/technical-snaptrade-commercial-integration-feasibi-2026-09-19/research.md#data-and-event-fit`
- **Requirement:** The experience must accommodate different update cadences: activities may update daily and omit time of day, recent orders are more current, and holdings freshness varies by plan and brokerage. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/research/technical-snaptrade-commercial-integration-feasibi-2026-09-19/research.md#data-and-event-fit`
- **Requirement:** When a brokerage connection is disabled, live data stops until reauthorization; the UI must expose an explicit degraded/reauthorization condition rather than silently presenting stale data as current. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#trends-and-constraints-that-matter-to-the-prd`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`

### Current SnapTrade API capability check (2026-09-19)

- Account details can supply institution, account-level sync status, and total market value; one brokerage connection can contain multiple accounts. Positions exclude cash, while balances can contain separate entries for multiple currencies. The UI must not silently treat one account, one connection, and one currency as equivalent. [Account Data](https://docs.snaptrade.com/docs/account-data); [List account balances](https://docs.snaptrade.com/reference/Account%20Information/AccountInformation_getUserAccountBalance)
- Positions can include many instrument kinds plus symbol, description, listing/position currency, exchange, units, last-known price, cost basis, cash-equivalent status, and optional tax lots. Field availability and price freshness vary by brokerage. Optional tax lots are not a first-cut candidate-card dependency. [List all account positions](https://docs.snaptrade.com/reference/Account%20Information/AccountInformation_getAllAccountPositions)
- Percentage or amount performance is shown only when Findur has a defensible source/calculation basis, named period, currency treatment, coverage, and freshness. Missing price, cost basis, history, or FX policy produces an explicit unavailable/unsupported state rather than an estimate.
- Recent orders and historical activities are separate datasets. Orders cover a bounded recent window and may better represent intraday status; activities include security and non-security events but are cached/daily and delayed. UX labels them separately and never turns a date-only activity into a precise timestamp. [List account orders](https://docs.snaptrade.com/reference/Account%20Information/AccountInformation_getUserAccountOrders); [List account activities](https://docs.snaptrade.com/reference/Account%20Information/AccountInformation_getAccountActivities); [Syncing and Data Freshness](https://docs.snaptrade.com/docs/syncing)
- Holdings-update webhooks cover positions, balances, orders, total account value, and account detail, but their arrival does not prove that values changed. Dataset-specific timestamps and states remain visible. [Webhooks](https://docs.snaptrade.com/docs/webhooks)
- SnapTrade also offers a hosted, read-only MCP server for Personal users. It exposes curated account, balance, position, balance-history, order, activity, currency, exchange-rate, exchange, and security-type tools through user OAuth; it does not itself review or interpret a portfolio. Connector responses are delivered to the chosen AI provider, so Findur would still own model behavior, selected-account enforcement, evidence provenance, disclosure filtering, retention boundaries, and provider approval. Treat MCP as a future protected-owner AI evidence option, not as an assumed replacement for Findur's Commercial/OAuth production integration. [SnapTrade MCP Server](https://docs.snaptrade.com/docs/mcp-server); [Connector Privacy Notice](https://docs.snaptrade.com/docs/connector-privacy); [Build Your Personal Investing App with AI](https://docs.snaptrade.com/docs/build-with-ai)

### Dating safety and prototype boundaries

- **Requirement:** Safety controls ship with discovery, not only chat: age gating, blocking, reporting, moderation review/escalation, anti-harassment rules, and warnings/prohibitions covering money requests, investment solicitation, and coercion to move off-platform. Financial disclosure must disappear immediately for a blocked or reported viewer. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#trends-and-constraints-that-matter-to-the-prd`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`
- **Requirement:** Keep the first milestone narrow: one private live test portfolio, synthetic candidates, OAuth/read-only connection, freshness/disconnect states, and comprehension testing of trust boundaries. Any public or production launch needs separate security, privacy, moderation, identity/age-assurance, legal, and SnapTrade-authorization gates. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/research/technical-snaptrade-commercial-integration-feasibi-2026-09-19/research.md#executive-summary`

## Research implications for UX decisions

### User expectations

- **Research implication:** This is a high-trust dating experience, so users are likely to judge it on whether financial data changes matching meaningfully while remaining private, controlled, and non-exploitative. The UX should make the value exchange legible before asking for connection consent. This is inferred from the source's high-trust framing and differentiation test; it is not a tested user expectation. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#verdict`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`
- **Research implication:** Users need to understand three different concepts: data used privately for matching, data they choose to disclose, and the explanation a recipient sees. Treating these as one permission would conflict with the required separation of private inputs and visible disclosure. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`
- **Research implication:** Because connected brokerage data is broad, mutable, and not equivalent to net worth, users need provenance and freshness cues near derived insights rather than only in settings or policy text. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#trends-and-constraints-that-matter-to-the-prd`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/research/technical-snaptrade-commercial-integration-feasibi-2026-09-19/research.md#data-and-event-fit`
- **Research implication:** A visible private-data boundary—“only you can see this” versus “approved for matches”—would help users verify what a prospective match can see. The sources require this boundary but do not prescribe its component or wording. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/research/technical-snaptrade-commercial-integration-feasibi-2026-09-19/research.md#production-grade-build-constraints-and-future-product-boundary`

### Competitor patterns, anti-patterns, and differentiation opportunities

- **Pattern:** Score validates financial responsibility and compatibility as a dating signal; its original minimum-credit-score threshold is a precedent for eligibility gating. Its claims are company-reported and were not independently tested in the source. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#what-exists-and-how-it-is-positioned`
- **Pattern:** The League packages ambition through curation, exclusivity, paid waitlist bypass, and concierge matchmaking. It is an adjacent substitute rather than a portfolio-data analogue. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#what-exists-and-how-it-is-positioned`
- **Anti-pattern:** Credit thresholds, status gating, public wealth leaderboards, wealth badges, universal financial scores, and pay-to-access-a-person's financial data would collapse compatibility into status and weaken the consent-led proposition. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#verdict`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#what-exists-and-how-it-is-positioned`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`
- **Differentiation opportunity:** Center voluntary evidence, compatibility controls, coarse user-approved traits, recipient protection, and understandable “why this person appears” explanations. This distinguishes Findur from Score's threshold history, The League's exclusivity, and mainstream dating's ordinary filters. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#verdict`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#what-exists-and-how-it-is-positioned`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`
- **Evidence limit:** The scan found no independently verified scaled app publicly documenting SnapTrade-style brokerage connections as its primary discovery mechanism. This supports novelty, not demand; UX validation must test comprehension and desirability rather than treating the market gap as proof. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#evidence-limits-and-refresh-triggers`

### Security and privacy implications visible to users

- **Research implication:** Pre-connect disclosure should say that access is read-focused/read-only for the stated prototype, identify the categories used to derive compatibility, and avoid implying Findur can trade or see more than the granted scopes allow. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#trends-and-constraints-that-matter-to-the-prd`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/research/technical-snaptrade-commercial-integration-feasibi-2026-09-19/research.md#authentication-and-onboarding`
- **Research implication:** A user-facing data-control surface should expose connection status, last refresh, coverage, disclosure choices, disconnect/revoke, and deletion/retention consequences. The sources require these controls but do not decide whether they live in onboarding, profile setup, settings, or all three. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`
- **Research implication:** Safety warnings should be contextual to discovery and conversation—not buried in a general policy—because portfolio visibility may increase targeting incentives and the cited fraud pattern includes investment pitches, money requests, and pressure to move off-platform. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#trends-and-constraints-that-matter-to-the-prd`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`
- **Research implication:** Block/report confirmation should tell the reporter that the other person immediately loses access to their financial disclosure; this turns an invisible protection into an auditable trust promise. The confirmation treatment is inferred, while immediate suppression is explicit. `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-findur-2026-09-19/research-market-landscape.md#prd-implications`
