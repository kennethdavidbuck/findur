Findur — UX Design Validation Report

DESIGN.md + EXPERIENCE.md

The draft pair has strong structure, complete token resolution, strong visual-reference discipline, and unusually thorough state and accessibility coverage. It is not yet safe as a downstream implementation contract because the source-required incoming-interest asymmetry has no committed surface or flow, FR-22 is mapped without an acceptance flow, Candidate Card signals remain unresolved, and several privacy-decreasing transitions can be interpreted as fail-open.

The combined accessibility and trust lens found no critical defects and judged accessibility close to strong. The required fixes are targeted: make privacy reductions immediate and fail-closed, define deterministic focus and consent transitions, correct contrast mappings, and commit the missing load-bearing content and flows. This does not require speculative production moderation or identity systems.

Accessibility + trust review

review-accessibility-trust.md

Accessibility is adequate and close to strong. Trust, consent, privacy, and safety are adequate but unsafe at privacy-decreasing transitions. The following findings are deliberately limited to core load-bearing behavior.

high

Account selection lacks accessible selection/progress semantics

Account Inclusion Control / Flow 8
Fix: Specify a labelled checkbox group, Select all mixed state, unavailable reasons, draft/committed counts, and concise progress announcements.
high

Privacy invalidation lacks deterministic focus recovery

Routing / State / Accessibility
Fix: Focus the replacement recovery/status heading, explain removal, expose the next safe action, and restore cancel to its invoker.
high

Two-stage consent is ambiguous

Consent Panel / Data Consent / Flows 1 and 8
Fix: Pre-OAuth authorizes provider access and minimum account metadata only; post-OAuth selection confirmation authorizes Findur use and derivation.
high

Account-exclusion failure can fail open

Account inclusion state / Flow 8
Fix: Immediately suspend exclusions, invalidate outputs, gate Discovery during retry, and never restore the broader set. Failed additions may retain the narrower set.
high

Disclosure-downgrade failure can fail open

Disclosure / Flow 2
Fix: Suppress broader output immediately and retry persistence; only a separately confirmed upgrade may broaden visibility.
medium

Disclosure selection lacks saved-versus-preview semantics

Fix: Use a labelled single-select group and separate inspected preview from saved value with concise announcements.
medium

Revealed Photo lacks an accessible equivalent

Fix: Name the revealed image concisely or declare adjacent identity text equivalent; never infer appearance.
medium

Weak borders conflict with the 3:1 non-text contrast promise

Fix: Reserve weak borders for decoration and use strong tokens for essential boundaries and chart marks.
medium

OAuth results and gates lack initial-focus rules

Fix: Focus the updated route/result heading and avoid duplicate live announcements.
medium

Source-to-Experience Trace collapses permission branches

Fix: Branch into owner-only exact view, private matching input, and candidate-visible disclosure with provenance.
medium

Purge rule conflicts with re-selectable account inventory

Fix: Retain only minimal masked connection metadata; purge financial payloads and derived output.
medium

Screenshot and inference limits appear too late

Fix: Put the limitation in Disclosure Level preview before save, especially near Full Detail.
medium

Inclusion expansion lacks explicit scope confirmation

Fix: Apply one scoped confirmation to the draft account set before new retrieval and derivation.
