- (decision) For a fully set-up eligible owner, Discovery and the Swipe Deck are the default authenticated landing experience. - (decision) Approved authenticated IA: three primary areas—Discovery, Portfolio, and Profile. Discovery owns the Swipe Deck, preferences, and deck recovery; Portfolio owns the private Connected Portfolio, freshness, connection controls, and source-to-experience explanation; Profile owns personal profile, disclosure, complete preview, language/theme, and account settings. Mobile and desktop preserve the same hierarchy; public/legal surfaces remain outside the authenticated shell.
- (decision) Additive first-cut requirement: after SnapTrade OAuth, users explicitly select which connected accounts Findur may use. Default to no accounts selected; require at least one for Discovery; offer Select all; allow editing from Portfolio settings; and show coverage such as “Using 2 of 4 connected accounts.” Findur-level account inclusion is separate from Disclosure Level and from SnapTrade-level OAuth access. Selection changes recalculate derived signals and remove excluded-account data from caches, previews, and stale-while-revalidate results. UX copy must distinguish these layers. Architecture must verify whether SnapTrade supports provider-level account-granular authorization.

Source facts and derived signals are labelled distinctly. The bounded relevance explanation uses proximity, selected similar/diversified/complementary preference, one or two named derived traits, Disclosure Level, and freshness—never a score, guarantee, cohort, formula, or worth judgment.
